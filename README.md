**1. Hotel Management System (HMS) – Project overview**

A Hotel Management System is a web-based application designed to manage the daily operations of a hotel from one centralized platform.

Its main objectives are:
- Manage hotel rooms and room availability.
- Handle guest registration and reservations.
- Manage check-in and check-out.
- Track payments, invoices, and expenses.
- Manage employees and hotel departments.
- Handle restaurant and additional hotel services.
- Generate operational and financial reports.

**2. Recommended HMS modules**
  
Module	Main responsibilities
Dashboard	Occupancy, revenue, bookings, statistics
Room Management	Rooms, types, rates, availability
Reservation	Booking, modification, cancellation
Guest Management	Guest profiles and history
Check-in / Check-out	Guest arrival and departure
Billing & Payments	Invoices, payments, outstanding balances
Housekeeping	Room cleaning and maintenance status
Restaurant	Food orders and restaurant billing
Inventory	Supplies and stock management
Employee Management	Staff, departments, responsibilities
Reports	Revenue, occupancy, booking reports
User Management	Accounts, roles and permissions
Settings	Hotel information, tax, currency and configuration
